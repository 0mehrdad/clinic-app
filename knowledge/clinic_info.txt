Women's Health Clinic

Location:
25 Harley Street, London.

Opening Hours:
Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM
Saturday: 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM
Sunday: Closed.

Cancellation Policy:
Patients should cancel or reschedule appointments at least 24 hours before their appointment.

Late Arrivals:
Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may need to reschedule their appointment.

Gynaecology Consultation:
A standard gynaecology consultation lasts approximately 45 minutes. Patients should bring details of any medications they currently take and any relevant previous medical records.

Payment:
Payment can be made by debit card or credit card at the clinic.

Contact:
Patients can contact the clinic through the clinic assistant for appointment-related questions.
